Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a drone?

You can book a drone by clicking the 'Book Drone Spraying' button on the home screen. You will then be asked to mark your farm on the map and confirm the booking.

What are the payment methods?

For the initial phase, we only support 'Cash on Completion'. You can pay the operator directly after the service is completed.

How can I cancel a booking?

You can cancel a booking by contacting our customer support through the options provided on this page.
